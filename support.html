Support

How to use the app, and how to reach us.

To…Do this
Play or pauseTap the round Play button. It stays at the bottom of the screen.
Change the voiceUse Voice under the progress bar. The katha continues from the same place.
Go to a chapterTap Chapters, open a chapter, then choose a section.
Read the aartiTap Aarti & lyrics.
Stop playing laterUse Sleep: 15, 30 or 60 minutes, or the end of this section.
Stay on one pageTurn off Turn pages with audio. Playback pauses at the end of each section.
Watch the filmTap Watch on YouTube. It opens YouTube.

Common questions

Do I need internet? No. The pictures and both voices are inside the app. Only Watch on YouTube needs a connection.

Is the narrator a real person? No. The voices are synthesized with Google Gemini text-to-speech.

I noticed a mistake in a word or shloka. Thank you. Please report it below with the section number and the time shown on screen.

Contact

Tell us your device, iOS version and what happened. Report a problem or suggest an improvement.

GitHub reports are public. Please leave out personal information.
