About the narration

The narration is synthesized with Google Gemini text-to-speech. It is not a human narrator. The text was carefully checked against a traditional family recording of the katha. A few Sanskrit and aarti readings are still being reviewed by fluent listeners. The illustrations are AI-generated, inspired by traditional devotional prints.
